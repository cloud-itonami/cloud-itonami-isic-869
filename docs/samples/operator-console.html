Human-in-the-loop decisions

The graph pauses at :request-approval (interrupt-before) and only resumes when a dispatcher supplies a decision. Both outcomes are exercised in this run: one safety flag approved, one rejected.

A granted approval reaches the store only as :approved-by on the :committed fact. store/append-ledger! is called from the :commit and :hold terminal nodes only, so the :approval-requested and :approval-granted facts that the :decide and :request-approval nodes emit exist only in the run’s in-memory :audit channel and never persist — measured: 0 :approval-requested and 0 :approval-granted fact(s) in the ledger, against 1 :committed fact(s) that do carry an approver. A rejection does land, as :approval-rejected, but none of the 1 rejection fact(s) name the rejecting dispatcher — transportops.operation’s hold-fact does not copy (:by approval) onto the fact it writes, so “who rejected this?” is not recoverable from the store even though the resume call supplied it.
